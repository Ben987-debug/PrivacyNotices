Privacy Policy – kinPin

1. Introduction

kinPin ("the App") is a family shared-memory application that lets households post household updates ("spins"), hand off chores, track daily habits, and keep long-lived household facts in a shared vault.

This Privacy Policy explains what information kinPin may process, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, kinPin may process the following information:

  • Account information: Information used to create and manage your account, such as your username and other details you provide.
  • Household information: Household names, member names, avatars, seat keys, and access roles you create or join.
  • User-generated content: Spins, chore handoffs, check-ins, receipts, household facts, and other content you choose to create or share through the App.
  • App data: Information required to maintain your settings, preferences, reminders, and app functionality.
  • Notification tokens: Device push tokens used solely to deliver household reminders and updates you are part of.
  • Technical information: Basic device, diagnostic, or connection information may be processed by the app infrastructure when necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

kinPin will only process information that is necessary for its functionality or that you choose to provide.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by kinPin may be used to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts and households.
  • Enable household members to post, assign, and complete shared spins.
  • Deliver reminders and household updates, including push notifications.
  • Store user preferences and application state, including backups you export.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and proper operation of the App.
  • Respond to support requests and address technical issues.

4. Sharing of Information

kinPin does not sell users’ personal information.

Information may be shared with other members of your household when necessary to provide the features you choose to use, such as assigning a chore or posting a household update. Household creators choose per-item audiences (for example, who can see a vault anchor).

Information may also be processed by infrastructure or service providers that help operate the App. Such processing is limited to what is necessary for the relevant service.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

5. User Content and Visibility

Content you share in a household may be visible to members of that household according to the audience you choose. Limited-access members only see spins they created or that are assigned to them.

kinPin does not guarantee that another member will keep shared content confidential or refrain from taking screenshots or otherwise copying it.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the App's features, maintain accounts, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved. Stamped receipts stay on the household board until cleared from the spin itself.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by kinPin against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

kinPin is a family app and children may use it together with their household. Accounts for young children should be created and supervised by a parent or guardian, who controls the household, its members, and per-item audiences.

If you believe personal information has been provided inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may stop using kinPin at any time.

If the App provides a family-deletion feature, you may use it to remove the household from the device. Otherwise, you may contact us using the details provided below to request account or data deletion.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

10. Third-Party Services

kinPin may rely on third‑party infrastructure or services to provide its functionality, such as cloud hosting, databases, and push-notification delivery. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Where applicable, users should review the privacy policies of those providers.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as kinPin evolves or as legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the same public URL, with the updated revision date.
