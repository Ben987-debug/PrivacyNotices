JUT Privacy Policy

JUT respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information JUT collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

JUT may collect:

  • Your JUT username/ID and password.
  • Your profile color and app settings.
  • Your JUT connections and connection requests.
  • Your device information and push-notification token when needed to deliver Juts.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

JUT does not require your real name, phone number, or email address for basic account use.

Juts

Juts are designed to be temporary signals, not chat messages.

JUT does not maintain a permanent history of Juts. Signals are delivered to the intended recipient and are then discarded rather than stored as message history.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and authenticate accounts.
  • Manage connections.
  • Deliver Juts and related notifications.
  • Maintain service security and reliability.
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized activity.

We do not sell your personal information or use Jut content for advertising profiles.

Sharing

JUT may use trusted service providers for hosting, database services, and push notifications. These providers process information only as needed to provide the service.

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, our users, or others.

Security

Passwords are securely hashed and are not stored as plain text. We use reasonable technical measures to protect account and service data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and Deletion

Account and connection information may be retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period necessary for security and legal purposes.

Juts themselves are designed to be ephemeral and are not stored as permanent messages.

You may request deletion of your account and associated account data using the account-deletion option provided by JUT. Information that must be retained for legal or security purposes may be kept where required.

Permissions and Overlays

JUT may use Android notification and overlay capabilities to display Juts as part of its core functionality.

JUT does not read, record, or collect content from other applications displayed on your device.
