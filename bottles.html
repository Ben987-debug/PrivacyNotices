Privacy Policy – Bottles

1. Introduction

Bottles ("the App") is a social gifting application that allows users to share virtual Gestures, including hearts, chocolates, balloons, roses, and similar expressions, with other people.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Bottles may process, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Bottles may process the following information:

  • Account information: Information used to create and manage your account, such as your username and other details you provide.
  • User-generated content: Messages, notes, Gestures, and other content you choose to create or send through the App.
  • App data: Information required to maintain your settings, preferences, and app functionality.
  • Technical information: Basic device, diagnostic, or connection information may be processed by the app infrastructure when necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Bottles will only process information that is necessary for its functionality or that you choose to provide.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by Bottles may be used to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Enable users to send and receive Gestures.
  • Deliver messages and other user-initiated content.
  • Store user preferences and application state.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and proper operation of the App.
  • Respond to support requests and address technical issues.

4. Sharing of Information

Bottles does not sell users’ personal information.

Information may be shared with other users when necessary to provide the features you choose to use, such as sending a Gesture or sharing a note with a recipient.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure or service providers that help operate the App. Such processing is limited to what is necessary for the relevant service.

Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

5. User Content and Visibility

Content you choose to send to another user may be visible to that recipient. Before sending a Gesture or an accompanying note, please consider the information you are sharing.

Bottles does not guarantee that a recipient will keep received content confidential or refrain from taking screenshots or otherwise copying it.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the App's features, maintain accounts, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by Bottles against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Bottles is not intended to be used in violation of applicable age requirements or child‑protection laws. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in circumstances where such collection is prohibited.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may stop using Bottles at any time.

If the App provides an account deletion feature, you may use it to request deletion of your account and associated information. Otherwise, you may contact us using the details provided below to request account or data deletion.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

10. Third-Party Services

Bottles may rely on third‑party infrastructure or services to provide its functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Where applicable, users should review the privacy policies of those providers.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Bottles evolves or as legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the same public URL, with the updated revision date.
