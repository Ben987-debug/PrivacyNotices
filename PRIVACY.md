# Privacy Notice

Unchat is designed for group conversations where sender names and message timestamps are not displayed to other members.

### What We Collect

We collect information needed to operate Unchat, including your account information, profile details you provide, messages, group memberships, reactions, and device/session information.

### Anonymous Messaging

Your name is not shown to other group members when you send a message. However, Unchat may associate messages with your account internally for security, service operation, abuse prevention, and legal requirements.

### How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide and synchronize the service, secure accounts, prevent abuse, and maintain Unchat. **We do not sell your personal information or use your messages for advertising.**

### Sharing

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, database, authentication, storage, and other infrastructure needed to operate Unchat. We may also disclose information when legally required.

### Your Data

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data through the available account-deletion functionality.
